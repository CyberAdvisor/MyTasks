About

Why My Tasks

My Tasks is a calm, focused task manager for people who want their tasks organized without paying for a subscription or handing them to another company. It brings together the best ideas from popular task managers, adds a few of its own, and keeps everything in your own GitHub account.

What it holds to

  • Free for good. Every feature, on every device, with no paid tier.
  • No My Tasks server. The app runs on your devices and keeps your tasks in a gist in your own account.
  • Encrypted on your device. Your tasks are encrypted with your passphrase before they leave it. See Security.
  • Open source. Anyone can read the code, host their own copy or change it, under the MIT licence.
  • Simple rather than perfect. Practical choices over clever ones; what a web app can't do is set out plainly in Limitations.

Who makes it

Made by Michael Lines, a retired long-time IT developer and executive, who now enjoys creating utilities and apps for himself and sharing them with the world for free. For more on this and his other projects, including his blogs and books, see michaellines.fyi.

Get in touch

Questions and ideas are welcome in r/mytasksfyi on Reddit, and bugs in GitHub issues. If My Tasks helps you, you can support it on Ko-fi.
