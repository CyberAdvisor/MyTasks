My Tasks, Your Way

Organizing work and life with one simple app · by Michael Lines

Juggling a business, a family and everything else is hard to keep straight. My Tasks, Your Way shows how one simple app can hold all of it, without asking you to follow anyone's system.

The book follows Marcus Reyes, a self-employed tech-review podcaster with a small team and a busy family, through a week with My Tasks: capturing everything, organizing his life into areas and projects, keeping Today realistic, working with his team, letting routines run themselves and ending the week with a short review. It's not a manual. Each chapter ends with a few questions to help you shape the same ideas to your own life, and points to the Guide for the step-by-step details.
