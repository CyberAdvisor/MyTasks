Roadmap

Last updated November 24, 2026

What’s planned for My Tasks, grouped by milestone and the version it’s planned for, then a backlog of ideas. Items with a ✓ are done, with the version they arrived in. Plans can change; items marked research are being looked into, not promised.

Coming next

Reminders

  • Give a task a reminder time; the calendar helper on your Mac adds it to Apple Reminders, which alerts you on your iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch (Mac only for now)

Backlog

These items aren’t under active development, but may be considered in the future.

Storage

  • Other places to keep your tasks (research)

Natural-language entry

  • Type a task with its details in plain words, such as “Call Sam tomorrow”, and have them set for you, with a setting to turn it on or off

Completed

Milestone 19 · Several gists v1.11.0 to v1.11.3

  • Several gists on one device, such as Work and Personal, each with its own tasks, passphrase, name and colour v1.11.0
  • A band in the sidebar, and at the top of the lists on phones, to switch between gists; Add Gist…, Rename… and Disconnect This Gist in Settings v1.11.1
  • The calendar helper on your Mac writes your events to every gist v1.11.2
  • The calendars shown in each gist chosen separately, such as only your work calendar in Work v1.11.3
  • Several gists on this website: the Guide, Limitations and Privacy pages

Milestone 18 · Changes kept on two devices v1.10.0 to v1.10.2

  • Changes to different parts of the same task on two devices are all kept, such as one completing it and another adding a note v1.10.0
  • A device tells you when a change of yours was replaced by a later one from another device v1.10.1
  • While offline, the sidebar shows how many changes are waiting to sync v1.10.2
  • Sharing your tasks with people you trust, with its limits, on this website
  • Screenshots on this website retaken, showing Today’s Hide / Show
  • The book shows sharing, with a partner keeping her own area, and its pictures retaken (Book)

Milestone 17 · Tidier lists v1.9.0 to v1.9.3

  • Unscheduled shows a few tasks of each project or area, with Show All for the rest, and a setting for how many v1.9.0
  • Scheduled lists each day’s tasks by priority v1.9.1
  • A priority of None you choose holds while a task is in Today, so automatic priorities apply when it comes back v1.9.3

Milestone 16 · Formatted notes v1.8.0 to v1.8.4

  • Notes show bold, italic, lists and clickable links v1.8.0
  • Fixes on iPhone: links in notes open, solid bullets, long titles wrap v1.8.1
  • Clickable links in checklist items v1.8.2
  • Larger text on the Mac v1.8.3
  • An Apple Pencil drags only after a long press v1.8.4

Milestone 15 · New logo v1.7.0

  • A new logo: MT above a check, in teal, for the app, its icons and this website v1.7.0
  • Screenshots on this website retaken with the new logo
  • The book updated, with its pictures retaken (Book)

Milestone 14 · Repeating projects v1.6.0 to v1.6.2

  • Repeat a project as you repeat a task: each copy is the whole project, with its headings, tasks and checklists v1.6.0
  • The template: its rule and next dates, Show Previous Copy and Create Next Copy, and listed in Scheduled on its next date v1.6.0
  • Work ahead on the next copy from the template, and complete, pause or stop a repeating project v1.6.1
  • Import and the Things script bring repeating projects in v1.6.2
  • A Guide section on repeating tasks and projects (Guide)

Milestone 13 · Calendar events v1.5.0 to v1.5.4

  • Today’s and coming events from your Mac’s calendars, shown in Today and Scheduled on all your devices, through a small helper you run on your Mac v1.5.2
  • Click an event to see its details: when and where, the link, attendees and notes v1.5.2
  • A guide to setting up the helper (Guide) v1.5.2
  • Scheduled laid out by day for a week, then the rest of the month, months and years, with your events v1.5.2
  • The privacy, security and limitations pages brought up to date for calendar events

Milestone 12 · Import v1.4.0 to v1.4.4

  • One file format for bringing in tasks from other apps: areas, projects, headings, tasks, checklists and repeating tasks (Import from other apps) v1.4.0
  • Import… in Settings, adding to what you have v1.4.0
  • Archive: old completed items saved to a file and removed, to keep your gist small v1.4.2
  • What’s in your gist, in Settings › Sync: tasks and projects by state, and the file’s size v1.4.3
  • A script for your Mac that writes that file from Things 3 v1.4.4

Milestone 11 · Review fixes v1.3.0 to v1.3.15

  • A clear message when a change can’t be saved v1.3.0
  • Task names easier to read on phones v1.3.1
  • Reorder checklist items by dragging, on iPhone and iPad too v1.3.2 to v1.3.4
  • No pinch zoom in the iPhone app v1.3.3
  • The filter bar shows tags only v1.3.5
  • What you’re typing kept when you leave the app v1.3.6
  • Repeating tasks kept right when their project is completed or the task is duplicated v1.3.8
  • Matching menus, long titles that wrap on iPhone, and a filter in the Move dialog v1.3.9 to v1.3.11
  • Dates shown in one order, month then day; the calendar starts on Sunday v1.3.12
  • The same space above the first task everywhere, and Sync now from the sidebar v1.3.14
  • Deleted items cleaned up after 180 days v1.3.15
  • Behind-the-scenes tidying v1.3.7, v1.3.13

Milestone 10 · Power editing v1.2.0 to v1.2.20

  • A task’s heading shown after its project, such as “Vacation : Packing” v1.2.0
  • See where a task went when you move it out of the Inbox v1.2.1
  • Focus on one area, so only its projects and tasks show v1.2.3
  • Type dates in plain words, such as “next fri” v1.2.4
  • Undo and redo more than one change v1.2.7
  • Select several tasks and change their priority, context or project, or delete them v1.2.9
  • Repeating tasks, including completing or skipping the next one early v1.2.13
  • Drag tasks onto the sidebar’s lists and between Today’s groups, several at once v1.2.14
  • Swipe a task on iPhone and iPad: left to right for its start date, right to left to select v1.2.15
  • Nested tags, and a shortcut key for each tag v1.2.16
  • Speed with long lists, up to 5,000 tasks in all, with a warning as you near that limit v1.2.20

Milestone 9 · This website website only, with v1.1.19 and v1.1.20

  • A guide to hosting or changing your own copy
  • A page comparing My Tasks with other popular task managers
  • Help, suggestions and news in the r/mytasksfyi community on Reddit
  • A way to support My Tasks with a donation
  • Common questions on the Home page
  • Setup guides for GTD, PARA and the Eisenhower matrix
  • An About page
  • A simpler header, and a footer in three columns

Milestone 8 · Tidy and polish v1.1.0 to v1.1.18

  • Collapsible areas, and collapsible headings in projects
  • Empty-list illustrations, a new logo, and a sidebar you can resize
  • Print a list
  • Right-click menus for projects, areas and saved searches in the sidebar
  • A mark on each task showing when it’s planned: a star for Today, the day for a scheduled task, a dashed box for a deferred one
  • “When” is now called Start Date; a start date can’t be later than the deadline, and a task moves into Today on its deadline
  • A full check against the design, and testing on iPhone and iPad
  • Sort a project’s tasks by date
  • The full task menu on touch screens, as a sheet on phones
  • A cleaner look on Mac, iPad and iPhone: new sizes for the sidebar and rows, new dark colours, and a sidebar you can hide
  • Sequential projects: when a task is done, the next one moves into Today, with a priority and context you choose
  • Automated tests of the interface

Milestone 7 · Ready for release v0.7.1 to v1.0.0

  • An independent review of security and privacy, and its fixes
  • Back up and restore your tasks, in Settings

Milestone 6 · Finishing touches v0.4.1 to v0.7.0

  • Typing straight into a new task, and a final Empty Trash
  • This website, with its guide, limitations, privacy and security pages
  • Confirming the risks and limitations at setup, and links to this website from the app

Milestone 5 · Sync, install and hosting v0.1.0 to v0.4.0

  • Light and dark themes, and the Completed list
  • Encrypted sync between your devices through your own GitHub gist
  • Install as an app that works offline, hosted at mytasks.fyi

Milestone 4 · Drag and drop, keyboard and undo v0.1.0 to v0.2.2

  • Drag and drop in lists and the sidebar, keyboard shortcuts, and undo
  • Each task’s right-click menu, and the menus after each list’s title
  • Contexts, and grouping Today by project, priority or context

Milestones 1 to 3 · The foundations v0.1.0

  • Inbox, Today, Scheduled, Unscheduled, Deferred, Completed and Trash
  • Areas, projects, headings, tags, checklists, notes, dates and deadlines
  • Priorities, saved searches, search and Quick Entry
